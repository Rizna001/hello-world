## 📜 New Important Islamic Texts Added

### 1. **Salawat Tibb al-Quloob** (Medicine of Hearts) 💚
- **Complete Arabic text** with full diacritics
- **Author:** Imam Ahmad al-Dardir (d. 1204 AH/1786 CE)
- **Recommended recitation:** 100 times
- **Benefits:** Healing of hearts and bodies, light for eyes
- **Multiple versions** available (short and complete)

**Arabic Text:**
```
اللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ طِبِّ الْقُلُوبِ وَدَوَائِهَا 
وَعَافِيَةِ الْأَبْدَانِ وَشِفَائِهَا وَقُوتِ الْأَرْوَاحِ وَغِذَائِهَا 
وَنُورِ الْأَبْصَارِ وَضِيَائِهَا وَعَلَى آلِهِ وَصَحْبِهِ وَسَلِّمْ
```

**Translation:** "O Allah, send blessings upon our master Muhammad, the medicine of hearts and their cure, the health of bodies and their healing, the nourishment of souls and their food, the light of eyes and their illumination, and upon his family and companions, and grant them peace."

### 2. **Salawat al-Fatih** (The Opener) 🔑
- **Complete Arabic text** with full diacritics
- **Author:** Sheikh Ahmad al-Tijani (founder of Tijaniyyah order)
- **Recommended recitation:** 100 times
- **Benefits:** Opens closed doors, seals what came before

**Arabic Text:**
```
اللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ الْفَاتِحِ لِمَا أُغْلِقَ 
وَالْخَاتِمِ لِمَا سَبَقَ نَاصِرِ الْحَقِّ بِالْحَقِّ 
وَالْهَادِي إِلَى صِرَاطِكَ الْمُسْتَقِيمِ 
وَعَلَى آلِهِ حَقَّ قَدْرِهِ وَمِقْدَارِهِ الْعَظِيمِ
```

**Translation:** "O Allah, send blessings upon our master Muhammad, the opener of what was closed, the seal of what came before, the helper of truth by truth, and the guide to Your straight path, and upon his family with the right of his status and his great magnitude."

---

### 3. **Salawat al-Nariyya** (The Fiery Prayer) 🔥
- **Complete Arabic text** with full diacritics
- **Author:** Abul Mawahib Abdul Wahhab al-Tazi al-Fasi
- **Recommended recitation:** 100 times
- **Benefits:** Unties knots, relieves difficulties, fulfills needs

**Arabic Text:**
```
اللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ صَلَاةً كَامِلَةً وَسَلِّمْ سَلَامًا تَامًّا 
عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ الَّذِي تَنْحَلُّ بِهِ الْعُقَدُ 
وَتَنْفَرِجُ بِهِ الْكُرَبُ وَتُقْضَى بِهِ الْحَوَائِجُ 
وَتُنَالُ بِهِ الرَّغَائِبُ وَحُسْنُ الْخَوَاتِيمِ 
وَيُسْتَسْقَى الْغَمَامُ بِوَجْهِهِ الْكَرِيمِ 
وَعَلَى آلِهِ وَصَحْبِهِ وَسَلَّمْ
```

**Translation:** "O Allah, send complete blessings and perfect peace upon our master Muhammad, through whom knots are untied, difficulties are relieved, needs are fulfilled, desires are attained, and good endings are achieved, and by whose noble face rain is sought from the clouds, and upon his family and companions."

### 4. **Qaseedatul Burdah** (Poem of the Mantle) 📜
- **Chapter 1 complete** with all 12 verses
- **Author:** Imam Sharafuddin al-Busiri (d. 694 AH)
- **Format:** Beautiful verse-by-verse display
- **Features:**
  - Arabic text with full diacritics
  - Transliteration
  - Translations in all 10 languages
  - Verse numbers
  - Reading mode for immersive experience

**Opening Verse:**
```
أَمِنْ تَذَكُّرِ جِيرَانٍ بِذِي سَلَمِ ❁ مَزَجْتَ دَمْعًا جَرَى مِنْ مُقْلَةٍ بِدَمِ
```

**Translation:** "Is it from remembering neighbors at Dhū Salam that you shed tears admixed with blood from your eyes?"

### 5. **Dua for Laylatul Qadr** 🌙
- **Complete Arabic text**
- **Reference:** Tirmidhi 3513
- **Recommended recitation:** 100 times on Laylatul Qadr

**Arabic Text:**
```
اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّكَ عَفُوٌّ تُحِبُّ الْعَفْوَ فَاعْفُ عَنِّي
```

**Translation:** "O Allah, You are the Pardoner, You love to pardon, so pardon me."

---

### 6. **Dua of Istikhara** 🤲
- **Complete Arabic text**
- **Reference:** Bukhari 1176
- **Usage:** Before making important decisions

**Arabic Text:**
```
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْتَخِيرُكَ بِعِلْمِكَ وَأَسْتَقْدِرُكَ بِقُدْرَتِكَ 
وَأَسْأَلُكَ مِنْ فَضْلِكَ الْعَظِيمِ
```

**Translation:** "O Allah, I seek Your guidance by virtue of Your knowledge, and I seek ability by virtue of Your power, and I ask You of Your great bounty."
