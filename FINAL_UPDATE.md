## 🎯 How to Use New Features

### Set Reminder from Any Dua:
1. Open any dua (e.g., Rabbana #3)
2. Click the blue "Set Reminder" button
3. Dua text is automatically copied to clipboard
4. Modal opens with pre-filled title
5. Choose reminder time (5 min, 15 min, 30 min, 1 hr, 2 hr, Tomorrow)
6. Choose notification sound
7. Click "Set Reminder"
8. You'll receive notification at the set time with the dua text!

### Change Voice/Reciter:
1. Open any dua
2. Scroll to audio player
3. If multiple voices available, you'll see a dropdown
4. Click dropdown to see all available Arabic voices
5. Select your preferred voice
6. Click Play to hear with selected voice
