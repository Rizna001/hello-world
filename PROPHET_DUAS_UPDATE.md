- ✅ Search functionality
- ✅ Click to expand and see detailed meaning
- ✅ Purple/pink gradient theme

**Example Meanings:**
- **Adam (آدَمُ):** "Human/Earth" (from 'adim' - earth/soil)
- **Ibrahim (إِبْرَاهِيمُ):** "Father of many/Father of mercy" (exalted father)
- **Musa (مُوسَى):** "Drawn out/Saved from water" (from 'masa' - to draw out)
- **Muhammad (مُحَمَّدٌ):** "The praised one/The praiseworthy" (from 'hamida' - to praise)
- **Yusuf (يُوسُفُ):** "God increases/Allah adds" (from 'yasaf' - to add/increase)

## 🎯 How to Use

### Viewing Prophet Duas:
1. Go to **Categories**
2. Click **"25 Prophets (Asma ul Anbiya)"**
3. Click on any prophet (e.g., "Musa (AS) - Moses")
4. The prophet expands showing:
   - Arabic name
   - English/Tamil/Malayalam name
   - Title/description
   - Time period
   - Audio player for name recitation
5. Click **"Show Duas (X)"** button
6. See all authentic duas made by that prophet
7. Each dua has its own audio player

### Viewing Prophet Name Meanings:
1. Go to **Categories**
2. Click **"📚 Names of Prophets with Meanings"**
3. See all 25 prophets listed
4. Click any prophet to expand
5. See the **meaning** of their name in:
   - English
   - Tamil (if selected)
   - Malayalam (if selected)
6. Use search to find specific prophets
